## Executive Presentation Summary

- **Title:** MedSync — AI-Powered Precision Care, Wearable Telemetry & Allergy Intelligence
- **Team:** Parth Sharma & MedSync Engineering Team
- **Institution:** JECRC / JECRC University, Jaipur (JECRC Incubation & Innovation Cell)
- **Problem Statement:** Intercepting preventable Adverse Drug Reactions (ADRs), hidden excipient allergens in FMCG/medicines, siloed consumer smartwatch streams, and unconsented health data silos.
- **Key Modules:**
  1. Edge OCR Allergen Scanner (RapidOCR INT8 + Levenshtein Fuzzy Matcher)
  2. Clinical Decision Support (NIH RxNorm + Gemini 3.8 Flash + 1-Click Safe Alternative Swap)
  3. Real-Time Wearable Telemetry (1Hz BLE GATT + Sliding Z-Score Anomaly Engine + Beta-Blocker ADR)
  4. 30/60/90-Day Predictive Risk Stratification (Scikit-Learn Variance ML Pipeline)
  5. National Health Stack (ABDM HIU Consent Flow, Mobile OTP Biometrics, FHIR R4 360-Degree Longitudinal Timeline)
- **Compliance:** HL7 FHIR R4, NRCeS India Profiles, DISHA, DPDP Act 2023, Apache-2.0 License.
